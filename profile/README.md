**What Strovia does**

- **Generate:** Messages 1, 2 and 3 from your own Excel or CSV extract, with every error traced to the cell to fix.
- **Validate strictly:** schema, code lists, rules and every item in every list, for Releases 2026 and 2027 side by side.
- **Explain:** what is wrong, where, why it matters and how to fix it.
- **Convert and chunk:** incoming messages into your own XML, CSV or Excel layouts; large messages split at the size agreed with each counterparty.
- **Track the cycle:** what has arrived, what is late and what is missing this month.
- **Govern:** versioned mappings, approval by a second person and a full audit trail.

**Here on GitHub** we take part in the public discussion of the VB-PUO standard in its official repository,
[dma61/VBPUOdsk](https://github.com/dma61/VBPUOdsk/issues). Strovia's own code is private.
